## COACHING ADDITIONS — not in original printed text
- Straight Press Down: shoulder extension/adduction torque against straps with scapular control; likely latissimus dorsi and posterior deltoid contribution, trunk stabilizers to resist rib flare. Keep wrists neutral and avoid driving shoulders into anterior glide.
- Triceps Press Down: emphasize elbow extension by triceps brachii while maintaining upper arm relatively steady; avoid shrugging and compensatory lumbar extension.
- Keep hip/knee tabletop without forcefully imprinting the lumbar spine unless separately cued; avoid cervical strain. Regress by resting feet on footbar or reducing resistance only if equipment setup permits; these are instructor proposals, **not source variations**.
