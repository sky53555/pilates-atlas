**Title:** Back Rowing Series Prep / 2. Flat Back. **Why:** strengthen spinal erectors and back musculature; strengthen upper body. **Starting Position:** sit on the Reformer facing the straps, both legs straight, feet placed forward against the shoulder rests; arms reach forward holding the straps. 1. INHALE — Ready.
2. EXHALE — Elbow Flexion & Lean Back.
3. INHALE — Elbow Extension.
4. EXHALE — Biceps Curl.

6. EXHALE — Return to Starting Position.
