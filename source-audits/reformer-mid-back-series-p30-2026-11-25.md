## COACHING — instructor additions, not source
Both: hips and knees flexed approximately 90 degrees, no deliberate hip rotation, thighs steady, feet unsupported (no heel/ball contact), head supported and gaze upward. Avoid rib flare, excessive lumbar flattening, shoulder elevation and wrist gripping. Side Press Down: shoulder adduction toward hips, latissimus dorsi, teres major and pectoralis major assist; scapular stabilizers and abdominal wall maintain trunk. Elbows long without locking; return slowly against spring. Arm Circle: anterior deltoid during flexion, posterior deltoid during horizontal abduction, pectoralis major during horizontal adduction, latissimus/teres major during lowering; rotator cuff controls humeral head. Keep circle within pain-free range and strap tension consistent. Regression: smaller arm range, feet supported on footbar if safe with setup; progression only after stable trunk, gradual resistance adjustment. These are suggestions, not printed variations.
