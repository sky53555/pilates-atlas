## PRINTED SOURCE (not instructor additions)
**Why:** stability of trunk through abdominal/pelvic muscles; strengthening Shoulder Extensor; strengthening serratus anterior and scapular stabilizers.
**Starting Position:** kneel on carriage, face toward shoulder rests; grip straps in both hands with arms alongside the body, and look forward.
**Breathing & Movement Sequence:** INHALE Ready → EXHALE Shoulder Extension → INHALE Turn One's Head Sideways → EXHALE Look a Head [source spelling preserved] → INHALE Return to Starting Position.
**Movement Point:** Transverse Abdominis, Pelvic Floor; Neutral Spine; Shoulder Movement: Shoulder Extensor; Scapula Stabilizer.
**Watch Point:** prevent pelvis from rocking and trunk from leaning forward/backward; prevent carriage from shaking with movement; keep knees in front of shoulder rests; avoid shrugging and excessive shoulder extension.
**Variation:** Chest Expansion With Pulse.
