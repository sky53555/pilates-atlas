## PRINTED SOURCE — p.36
**Heading:** Sitting Arm Forward Footbar Series.
**Exercises:** 1. Straight Forward; 2. Hugging Arm.
**Why:** Strengthening upper-extremity muscles and stabilizing trunk/pelvis.
**Starting Position:** (1) Sit on carriage facing footbar; hold straps in hands, shoulder-width; lengthen spine; legs extended in front with feet supported on footbar; (2) with arms extended forward at shoulder height, keep shoulder blades stable and bend elbows comfortably.
**Breathing / Movement Sequence:** INHALE Ready → EXHALE Shoulder Flexion (1) / Horizontal Adduct (2) → INHALE Return to Starting Position.
**Movement Point:** Transverse Abdominis / Pelvic Floor; Neutral Spine; Shoulder Flexion & Horizontal Abduction / Adduction; Scapula Stabilizer.
**Watch Point:** Maintain spinal length; avoid shoulders lifting and neck/shoulder tension; stabilize scapula during movement; avoid rib cage rocking; keep carriage stable without losing balance. Source warns against excessive wrist bending and excessive hand grip. No separately printed Variation block on p.36.
