Exercise Encyclopedia (필라테스 동작 백과)

매트·리포머·체어·타워·배럴·소도구 30개 동작 · 목적·진행·수정·안전

EXERCISE 01 · Mat (매트)

The Hundred (헌드레드)

Start Position (시작 자세)

Supine (바로 누움)

Primary Goal (주요 목표)

Trunk endurance (몸통 지구력), breathing coordination (호흡 협응)

Progression (진행 방법)

Prepare with knees bent and head resting; progress arm pulses and lever length gradually

Teaching Cue (지도 큐)

“Keep the ribs comfortable while the arms pulse gently”

Modification & Safety (수정·안전)

Avoid breath holding and neck strain; head can remain supported

EXERCISE 02 · Mat (매트)

Roll Up (롤업)

Start Position (시작 자세)

Supine (바로 누움)

Primary Goal (주요 목표)

Segmental spinal flexion (분절 척추 굽힘), control (조절)

Progression (진행 방법)

Start with bent-knee assisted roll back; progress range before leverage

Teaching Cue (지도 큐)

“Curl gradually rather than pulling with the neck”

Modification & Safety (수정·안전)

Do not force spinal flexion in symptomatic individuals

EXERCISE 03 · Mat (매트)

Single Leg Stretch (싱글 레그 스트레치)

Start Position (시작 자세)

Supine curl (누운 굽힘)

Primary Goal (주요 목표)

Trunk control (몸통 조절), alternating hip motion (교대 고관절 움직임)

Progression (진행 방법)

Head down and small leg range before long lever

Teaching Cue (지도 큐)

“Exchange legs without rushing”

Modification & Safety (수정·안전)

Reduce abdominal demand if breath is held or back discomfort appears

EXERCISE 04 · Mat (매트)

Double Leg Stretch (더블 레그 스트레치)

Start Position (시작 자세)

Supine curl (누운 굽힘)

Primary Goal (주요 목표)

Trunk endurance (몸통 지구력), limb dissociation (사지 분리)

Progression (진행 방법)

Arms only, then legs only, then combined reach

Teaching Cue (지도 큐)

“Reach only as far as trunk control allows”

Modification & Safety (수정·안전)

Reduce lever length for low-back discomfort

EXERCISE 05 · Mat (매트)

Spine Stretch Forward (스파인 스트레치 포워드)

Start Position (시작 자세)

Seated (앉음)

Primary Goal (주요 목표)

Spinal flexion (척추 굽힘), posterior-chain mobility (후방 가동성)

Progression (진행 방법)

Sit on folded towel; bend knees if necessary

Teaching Cue (지도 큐)

“Reach the crown forward in a smooth arc”

Modification & Safety (수정·안전)

Avoid pulling aggressively into end range

EXERCISE 06 · Mat (매트)

Start Position (시작 자세)
